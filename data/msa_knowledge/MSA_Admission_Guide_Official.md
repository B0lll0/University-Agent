## 2. How to Apply to MSA University (6 Official Steps)
**خطوات التقديم والالتحاق بجامعة MSA**

1. **Step 1 — Submit Online Application:** Fill out the online application form on the MSA Admissions portal.
2. **Step 2 — Check Email for Application Payment Details:** Follow the emailed payment instructions.
3. **Step 3 — ELPT Exam & Profiling Questionnaire:** Sit for the English Language Placement Test (if not exempt) and complete the profiling questionnaire.
4. **Step 4 — Personal Interview & Parents' Involvement Session:** Receive an email to schedule the student's faculty interview/club activity and mandatory in-person Parents' Involvement Program session.
5. **Step 5 — Conditional Offer Letter:** Check email for the official conditional offer letter and payment order to complete tuition payment on campus.
6. **Step 6 — Submit Original Required Documents:** Submit all original authenticated documents at the MSA Admission Office for verification.

**New Applicant Dashboard:** Log in with the ID number and password sent via email to view applied faculties, track payment/discount status, print or cancel applications, upload photos, read sent emails, or add new applications.

## 3. English Language Placement Test (ELPT) Rules & Exemptions
**قواعد اختبار تحديد مستوى اللغة الإنجليزية وحالات الإعفاء**

- **On-Campus Testing Hours:** Saturday to Thursday, **8:30 AM to 12:00 PM**.
- **Online Exam:** Must be reserved in advance via Hotline `16672` or Facebook Messenger `@MSAUniversityOfficial`. Students may take the ELPT online **ONE TIME ONLY**. Any retake/reset MUST be taken **on campus at MSA University**.
- **Mandatory Requirement:** New applicants must achieve the required English proficiency level for their chosen faculty.
- **Certificates Exempt from ELPT** (email certificate copy to `admission@msa.edu.eg` after registration):
  - **IELTS & TOEFL** certificate holders
  - **IGCSE** certificate holders with English grade **minimum B or 6**
  - **IB (International Baccalaureate) & Canadian Diploma** certificate holders
  - **American Diploma** holders with:
    - **EST I:** English score **550 or above**
    - **SAT I:** Reading + Writing score **500 or above**
    - **ACT I:** English + Reading score **32 or above**

## 4. Admission for Transfer Students (التحويل من جامعات أخرى)
- **CRITICAL RULE:** Transfer students **MUST NOT withdraw** their file from their current university until they receive explicit final approval and acceptance from an official MSA Admission Officer.
- **Minimum GPA Required:**
  - **Faculty of Dentistry:** Minimum cumulative GPA of **3.0 to 3.5**.
  - **All other MSA faculties:** Minimum cumulative GPA of **2.0**.
- **Required Transfer Documents & Steps:**
  - Full copy of high school documents (to verify eligibility).
  - Good Conduct letter (خطاب حسن سير وسلوك) from previous university.
  - Military service forms for Egyptian males (`Namouzag 2 + 6 Gond`).
  - Copy of National ID card (and passport for non-Egyptians).
  - Official university transcript + detailed original stamped course descriptions for all studied subjects.
  - Complete online application, Faculty Interview, and MSA ELPT.
  - Submit official withdrawal letter (خطاب سحب) ONLY after final admission & faculty approval.

## 5. Admission for International Students (الطلاب العرب والأجانب)
- All international (non-Egyptian) students must apply through the **Study in Egypt (إدرس في مصر)** platform in 3 steps:
  1. Create an account and upload final original stamped high school certificates.
  2. Receive the official **Nomination Letter** after certificate review and score calculation.
  3. Submit original stamped documents to MSA University and pay tuition fees in USD on campus at NBE.
- **MSA Key Facts & UK Partners:**
  - Founded in 1996, 32,000+ graduates, 36 student clubs, 11 faculties, dual Egyptian + UK degrees.
  - **University of Greenwich (UK) validates 6 faculties:** Engineering, Pharmacy, Computer Science, Biotechnology, Management Sciences, Nutrition & Food Technology.
  - **University of Bedfordshire (UK) validates 4 faculties:** Mass Communication, Languages, Arts & Design, Physical Therapy.
  - **International Partners:** University of Greenwich (UK), University of Bedfordshire (UK), Temple University (USA), Yunnan University (China), ACCA Middle East.

## 6. Official Admission Documents Required for All Certificates
**المستندات الرسمية المطلوبة للتقديم بجامعة MSA**

### General Documents Required for Every Applicant
- **5 recent personal photos** (عدد 5 صور شخصية).
- **Birth Certificate:** 2 recent original computerized birth certificates (Egyptians) OR birth certificate authenticated by student's Embassy and Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs (non-Egyptians).
- **Identification:** Copy of student's and parent's National ID + copy of student's passport (if available).
- **Egyptian Male Students Only:** Military service forms **Namouzag 2 + (6 or 7) Gond** (نموذج 2 + 6 أو 7 جند).

### Certificate-Specific Authentication Requirements
- **Egyptian Thanaweya Amma & Azharia:** Original certificate stamped by the Egyptian Ministry of Education (+ Sports Achievement certificate stamped by Ministry of Youth and Sports, if available).
- **STEM Schools:** Original STEM certificate bearing the official eagle seal (ختم النسر).
- **IGCSE (from Egypt):** Original IGCSE certificates authenticated by the British Council, Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and General Administration of Examinations + proof of 12 years of schooling (or 3rd Prep + 3 yrs / 6th Primary + 6 yrs, or 11-year Ministry proof) + Arabic, Religion & Civics certificate. *(From abroad: stamped by school, MOE, MOFA of issuing country, and Egyptian Embassy).*
- **American Diploma (from Egypt):** Final official transcript + graduation certificate stamped by school and General Administration of Examinations + Original **ACT 1/2** (American Academy + MOE) OR **EST 1/2** (General Admin of Examinations) OR **SAT 1** (AMIDEAST + Egyptian MOFA + General Admin of Examinations) + EST/SAT/ACT account login credentials + proof of 12 years schooling (eagle seal) + school registration statement + Arabic, Religion & Civics results. *(From abroad: also requires original AMIDEAST school accreditation letter).*
- **Abitur (German):** Original certificate stamped by school, German Embassy, Egyptian MOFA, and General Administration of Examinations + 12-year schooling proof + Arabic & Religion certificate.
- **French Baccalaureate:** Original + certified copy stamped by school, French accreditation center, French Embassy, Egyptian MOFA, and General Administration of Examinations + 12-year proof (French Brevet accepted) + Arabic/Religion/Civics + Parent's Involvement & Student Interview certificates.
- **International Baccalaureate (IB):** Original final transcript & diploma in English stamped in Geneva (Switzerland), Egyptian Embassy in Switzerland, General Administration of Examinations, IBSEA, and MOE + 12-year proof + Arabic/Religion/Civics.
- **Canadian Diploma:** Original certificate stamped by school, Canadian educational authority, Canadian Embassy, Egyptian MOFA, and General Administration of Examinations + 12-year proof + Arabic & Religion.
- **CNISE (Nile International):** Original CNISE slips authenticated by General Administration of Examinations + 12-year proof + MOE high school exam results.
- **Arab Certificates (Saudi Mokararat, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, UAE, Qatar, Yemen):** Original certificate authenticated by school, issuing country's MOE, issuing country's MOFA, and Egyptian Embassy (Saudi requires Qeyas login credentials; Kuwait requires passport entry/exit stamps & residence permit; Bahrain requires Grades 10, 11 & 12; UAE medical sector requires stamped Grade 11; Yemen requires Grades 9, 10 & 11 authenticated by Egyptian Cultural Attaché).

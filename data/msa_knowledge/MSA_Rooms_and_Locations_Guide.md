# MSA University Room Numbering System and Building Map

Q: How do room numbers and building letters work at MSA University?
A: MSA University room codes combine a building letter and a three digit number where the first digit indicates the floor and the last two digits indicate the room number: 1xx is the First floor at ground level (no stairs or elevator needed), 2xx is the Second floor upstairs, 3xx is the Third floor, and 4xx is the Fourth floor. For example, Room C111 is in Building C on the ground level First floor (Room 11), and Room G204 is in Building G on the Second floor (Room 04).

# Complete MSA Building Letters and Numbered Blocks

Q: What is the mapping between MSA lettered buildings (A, B, C, D, E, G, H, K, M, SSB) and numbered buildings (1 to 13)?
A: Building A (Building 1) houses University Administration, Faculty of Languages, Faculty of Mass Communication, and Faculty of Management Sciences. Building B (Building 2 / SSB) is the Student Services Building housing Student Affairs, registration, student activities, seminar rooms, and the campus Cinema Hall. Building C (Building 3) houses the Faculty of Pharmacy, Faculty of Engineering, Faculty of Biotechnology, Faculty of Computer Science, and Faculty of Dentistry. Building D (Building 4) houses specialized scientific laboratories and Dentistry Outpatient Clinics. Building E (Building 5) houses the Faculty of Arts and Design, classrooms, and offices. Building 6 houses Engineering and Design Workshops. Building 7 houses the Main University Library and the MSA Gym. Building G houses lecture halls, auditoriums, computer labs, staff rooms, Arts and Design exhibition spaces, and the 1,150 seat MSA Opera House. Building H is the Research Center housing dental outpatient clinics, operation theaters, radiology rooms, sterilization units, and computer labs. Building K houses lecture halls, computer labs, and Pharmacy and Computer Science sessions including Room K108 (Bus Room), Room K110, and Room K312. Building M houses clinical training rooms and workshops primarily used by the Faculty of Physical Therapy including Room M108. Building 11 is the Dentistry Extension, Building 12 is the Main Theater and Conference Halls, and Buildings 8, 9, 10, and 13 are expansion blocks.

# Specific Rooms and Campus Facility Locations

Q: Where is Room K108, the Bus Room, the Library, the Gym, the Opera House, Student Affairs (SSB), and the Dental Clinics?
A: Room K108 (Building K, First floor) is the official MSA Bus Subscription Room. The Main University Library and MSA Gym are in Building 7. The 1,150 seat Opera House is in Building G. Student Affairs, registration services, and the campus Cinema Hall are in Building 2 (Building B / SSB — Student Services Building). Dentistry Outpatient Clinics and scientific labs are in Building 4 (Building D), Building H (Research Center), and Building 11 (Dentistry Extension). Engineering workshops are in Building 6.

# نظام ترقيم القاعات والغرف ودليل المباني في جامعة MSA

س: إزاي أعرف مكان القاعة أو الغرفة في جامعة MSA من رقمها؟
ج: يعتمد ترقيم القاعات في جامعة MSA على حرف المبنى متبوعاً بثلاثة أرقام، حيث يدل الرقم الأول على الدور والرقمان الأخيران على رقم الغرفة: 0xx تعني الدور الأرضي، و1xx تعني الدور الأول، و2xx تعني الدور الثاني، و3xx تعني الدور الثالث، و4xx تعني الدور الرابع. مثلاً غرفة K108 موجودة في مبنى K الدور الأول غرفة 08 (مكتب اشتراكات الأتوبيسات)، وقاعة G204 في مبنى G الدور الثاني غرفة 04.

س: فين مكان المكتبة، الجيم، شؤون الطلاب، الأوبرا، مكتب الأتوبيس، الكليات، وعيادات الأسنان في MSA؟
ج: شؤون الطلاب والأنشطة والسينما في مبنى 2 (مبنى B / SSB). الإدارة واللغات والإعلام والإدارة في مبنى 1 (مبنى A). الهندسة والحاسبات والصيدلة والتكنولوجيا الحيوية وطب الأسنان في مبنى 3 (مبنى C). المعامل العلمية وعيادات الأسنان في مبنى 4 (مبنى D) ومركز الأبحاث مبنى H وتوسعة الأسنان مبنى 11. كلية الفنون والتصميم في مبنى 5 (مبنى E). ورش الهندسة في مبنى 6. المكتبة الرئيسية وجيم الجامعة في مبنى 7. دار الأوبرا بسعة 1150 مقعداً وقاعات المحاضرات ومعامل الكمبيوتر في مبنى G. قاعات ومعامل الحاسبات والصيدلة ومكتب الأتوبيس (غرفة K108) في مبنى K. قاعات وورش العلاج الطبيعي (مثل M108) في مبنى M. المسرح الرئيسي وقاعات المؤتمرات في مبنى 12.

# MSA University Attendance and Class Arrival Rules

Q: What is the attendance rule and absence limit at MSA University?
A: Students at MSA University must maintain a minimum attendance rate of 75 percent across all lectures, tutorials, and laboratories for each module. Falling below 75 percent attendance (even with medical or emergency absences) leads to deprivation from the final exam and automatic failure in that module. For regular classes, late entry is permitted only during the first 5 minutes; after 5 minutes the student is marked absent.

# Exam Entry Rules and Academic Integrity

Q: What are the late arrival rules for midterm and final exams at MSA University?
A: For 1.5 hour midterm exams, students are allowed entry only within the first 15 minutes. For 3 hour final exams, entry is permitted up to the first 30 minutes. Students must bring their official MSA University Student ID card to attend examinations and enter campus facilities.

Q: What are the plagiarism and academic misconduct rules at MSA University?
A: MSA University enforces strict academic integrity rules. Plagiarism includes quoting words or paragraphs without proper citation, paraphrasing someone else's ideas without acknowledgment, borrowing unacknowledged data, or copying another student's assignments, computer code, or exam answers. Suspected violations trigger a formal faculty investigation committee.

# Credit Hours, GPA Scale, and Academic Probation

Q: How do credit hours, GPA, and academic probation work at MSA University?
A: MSA University follows a 4.0 letter grading scale (A through D, Fail = 0). Bachelor programs typically require at least 140 credit hours across four academic years (8 semesters), depending on the faculty. In the summer semester, students may register for a maximum of 7 credit hours. A minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 is required for good academic standing and graduation. Students whose cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 are placed on academic probation with restricted course load until their cumulative GPA returns to 2.0 or higher. When repeating a module, only the highest earned grade counts toward the cumulative GPA.

# Registration, Financial Clearance, and English Placement Test (ELPT)

Q: What is required for course registration and the English Language Placement Test (ELPT) at MSA University?
A: Before registering courses during the academic calendar advising period, students must register their bank tuition payment receipts with the MSA Accounting Department. All new applicants must take the English Language Placement Test (ELPT) unless exempt with valid IELTS, TOEFL, IGCSE English grade B or 6+, IB, Canadian diploma, or qualifying SAT, ACT, or EST scores. The online ELPT can be taken one time only; any retake or reset must be taken physically on campus.

# Admission Requirements and Required Documents at MSA University

Q: What general documents are required for admission to MSA University?
A: All new applicants submit 5 recent passport photos, 2 original computerized Egyptian birth certificates (or authenticated birth certificates for non Egyptians), copies of student and parent National ID cards, passport copy if available, and for Egyptian male students military service Form 2 Gond plus Form 6 or 7 Gond.

Q: What are the certificate requirements for Thanaweya Amma, IGCSE, and American Diploma at MSA University?
A: Thanaweya Amma students submit their original stamped certificate matching the required faculty section (Scientific Math for Engineering; Scientific Science for Pharmacy, Dentistry, and Physical Therapy). IGCSE students submit original certificates authenticated by the British Council, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and Ministry of Education, proof of 12 years of schooling, and stamped Arabic, Religion, and Civics certificates, with 8 subjects (up to 9 for some faculties), minimum grade C (OL) or D (AL/ASL), within 4 successive years. American Diploma students submit an authenticated official transcript with 8 to 9 subjects (at least 4 from Grade 12, max 2 from Grade 10, minimum 1 credit each), SAT 1 or EST 1 score of at least 800 (or ACT 1 score of 14), and SAT 2, EST 2, or ACT 2 score of at least 900 for faculties requiring subject tests.

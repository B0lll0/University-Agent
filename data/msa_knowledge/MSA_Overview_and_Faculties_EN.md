# MSA University Overview and Location

Q: What is MSA University and where is it located?
A: October University for Modern Sciences and Arts (MSA University) is a leading private university located in 6th of October City, Giza, Egypt, at the intersection of the 26th of July Mehwar Road and Wahat Road, about 30 km from central Cairo. The official hotline is 16672 (international landline 002 0216672), support email is info@msa.edu.eg, and the website is msa.edu.eg.

# Dual British Degree Partnerships

Q: Which British universities partner with MSA University for dual degrees?
A: MSA University operates a dual degree system where graduates earn both an Egyptian bachelor degree accredited by the Supreme Council of Universities and a British bachelor degree validated by either the University of Greenwich or the University of Bedfordshire, enabling direct postgraduate study in the UK without traditional degree equivalence.

Q: Which MSA faculties are partnered with the University of Greenwich?
A: Six faculties partner with the University of Greenwich: Faculty of Engineering, Faculty of Pharmacy, Faculty of Computer Sciences, Faculty of Biotechnology, Faculty of Management Sciences, and Faculty of Nutrition and Food Technology.

Q: Which MSA faculties are partnered with the University of Bedfordshire?
A: Four faculties partner with the University of Bedfordshire: Faculty of Mass Communication, Faculty of Languages, Faculty of Arts and Design, and Faculty of Physical Therapy. The Faculty of Oral and Dental Medicine operates under the main Egyptian accreditation system within MSA University.

# University Transportation and Bus Services

Q: Does MSA University provide campus buses and where do I subscribe?
A: Yes. MSA University operates a private bus fleet covering Greater Cairo and Giza including 6th of October, Sheikh Zayed, Palm Hills, Dream Park, Al Motamayez, Hadayek El Ahram, Haram, Faisal, Mohandessin, Maadi, Mokattam, Nasr City, Heliopolis, Shubra, New Cairo, Al Rehab, Madinaty, and Helwan. Students manage bus subscriptions at the official Bus Room in Room K108.

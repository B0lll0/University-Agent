# MSA University Campus Layout and Architectural Design

Q: What is the architectural design and campus layout of MSA University in 6th of October?
A: MSA University occupies a 50 acre permanent campus in 6th of October City designed around open green landscapes, pedestrian courtyards, natural ventilation, and eco friendly sustainable architecture. Campus architectural highlights include the MSA Center of Earth constructed with Compressed Stabilized Earth Blocks (CSEB), the landmark 1,150 seat Opera House in Building G, the El Degwi Mosque, 31 cafeterias and food courts, and comprehensive sports grounds including football, basketball, volleyball, padel courts, and the MSA Gym.

# MSA University Buildings Directory (Buildings 1 to 13, SSB, and Building G)

Q: What is located in Building 1, Building 2 (SSB), and Building 3 at MSA University?
A: Building 1 houses University Administration, the Faculty of Languages, the Faculty of Mass Communication, and the Faculty of Management Sciences. Building 2 is the Student Services Building (SSB), serving as the central hub for student affairs, student activities, career events like Build Your Future, seminar spaces, and the renovated campus Cinema Hall. Building 3 houses the major scientific and technical faculties: Faculty of Pharmacy, Faculty of Engineering, Faculty of Biotechnology, Faculty of Computer Science, and Faculty of Dentistry.

Q: What is located in Building 4, Building 5, Building 6, and Building 7 at MSA University?
A: Building 4 houses specialized scientific research laboratories and the Faculty of Dentistry Outpatient Clinics. Building 5 houses the Faculty of Arts and Design (including Interior Architecture, Graphics, and Media Arts), classrooms, and offices. Building 6 houses Engineering and Design technical workshops. Building 7 houses the Main University Library and the MSA Gymnasium and sports complex.

Q: What are Buildings 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, Building G, and Room K108 at MSA University?
A: Building 11 is the dedicated extension for the Faculty of Dentistry. Building 12 houses the Main Theater and large conference and seminar rooms. Buildings 8, 9, 10, and 13 are designated for educational expansion. Building G is a major campus landmark housing lecture halls, auditoriums, computer labs, staff offices, Arts and Design exhibition spaces, and the 1,150 seat MSA Opera House indoor theater. Room K108 is the official Bus Room for university transportation subscriptions.

# Architectural Systems Engineering (ASE) Department at MSA University

Q: What does the Architecture Department at MSA University study and what facilities does it have?
A: The Faculty of Engineering offers a Bachelor degree in Architectural Systems Engineering (ASE) validated with the University of Greenwich. Its studio based curriculum covers Architectural Design, History and Theories of Architecture, Urban Planning, Landscape Design, Interior Design, Environmental Control Systems, Computer Graphics, and Construction Management. Students work in dedicated architectural design studios, digital computer labs, engineering workshops in Building 6, and sustainable architecture spaces such as the MSA Center of Earth.

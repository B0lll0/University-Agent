## 3. Official Tuition Payment Rules, Annual Increase & Refund Policy
**تعليمات هامة بشأن سداد المصروفات والاسترداد**

- **Payment Order Required:** Students must NOT pay tuition fees until they receive an official **Payment Order (إذن الدفع)** from the Admission and Registration Office.
- **On-Campus Payment Only (New Applicants):** New Egyptian applicants pay tuition in **Egyptian Pounds (EGP)** on campus only. Non-Egyptian/international students must pay in **US Dollars (USD)** through the **National Bank of Egypt (NBE — البنك الأهلي المصري)** branch on campus ONLY (Account Number: `1823060351711000017` USD). Bank transfers from abroad are not accepted.
- **British Validation Fees:** Paid in the **first semester of each academic year** in **Pound Sterling (£)** or its equivalent in **US Dollars ($)** as rated by the MSA Financial Department.
- **Category Permanence:** The assigned fee category applies **till graduation** for all new and external transfer students.
- **Annual Increase:** Full tuition fees are subject to an annual increase of **10% to 15%** prior to applied categories.
- **Application Cancellation & Refund Policy:** If an applicant cancels their application, **2,000 EGP** is deducted as application fees, and the remaining balance is refunded via bank transfer starting **October 1** (regardless of original payment date). The deadline to request a refund is **August 31**; after August 31, paid amounts are non-refundable.

## 4. Official MSA Scholarships Programs & Discounts
**برامج المنح الدراسية والخصومات بجامعة MSA**

Special scholarships can be combined and are calculated from the student's category fees (except Legacy Program + Similar Education Parent discounts, which cannot be combined together):

1. **15% Similar Education Parents Scholarship (Till Graduation):**
   - Granted if a parent graduated from the same or a similar faculty at any accredited Egyptian or international university. Valid for all years of study and **not conditional on GPA**.
   - Required document: Parent's graduation certificate (شهادة تخرج أحد الوالدين).
   - **Eligible parent faculties by MSA Faculty:**
     - **Dentistry:** Medicine (طب بشري), Dentistry (طب الأسنان).
     - **Physical Therapy & Pharmacy:** Medicine, Dentistry, Physical Therapy, Pharmacy, Veterinary Medicine.
     - **Biotechnology:** Medicine, Dentistry, Physical Therapy, Pharmacy, Veterinary Medicine, Engineering (all), Agriculture (all), Education (Science/Math), Science (all), Military & Police Officers, Judiciary graduates.
     - **Nutrition & Food Technology:** Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Physical Therapy, Science, Agriculture.
     - **Engineering:** Engineering (all), Higher Engineering Institutes (5-year), Higher Computer Institutes, Computer Science, Computers & AI, Applied Arts, Fine Arts.
     - **Computer Science:** Engineering (all), Computer Science, Science (Math & CS), Computers & AI, Higher Computer Institutes.
     - **Arts & Design:** Applied Arts, Fine Arts, Engineering, Art Education, Higher Cinema Institute, Higher Institute of Theatrical Arts, Specific Education, Mass Communication.
     - **Management Sciences:** Commerce, Computer Science, MBA, Higher Business Institutes, Cooperation Institute (Business), Al-Alsun, Economics & Political Science, Arts, Management Technology & IS, Mass Communication.
     - **Mass Communication:** Commerce, MBA, Higher Business Institutes, Cooperation Institute (Business), Arts, Fine Arts, Applied Arts, Higher Cinema Institute, Theatrical Arts, Mass Communication, Al-Alsun.
     - **Languages:** Commerce, MBA, Higher Business Institutes, Cooperation Institute (Business), Al-Alsun, Arts, Archaeology & Languages, Education, Specific Education, Art Education, Dar El-Uloom, Academy of Arts, Cinema, Theatrical Arts, Tourism & Hotels.
2. **15% Legacy Program Scholarship (Till Graduation):** For sons and daughters of MSA Alumni (cannot be combined with Similar Education Parent discount).
3. **10% Dar El Tarbiah & IKC Schools Scholarship (Till Graduation):** For graduates of Dar El Tarbiah and IKC Schools.
4. **10% Sports Scholarship (First Semester + Renewable):** Granted to national sports champions in the first semester, continuing in following semesters if the student represents MSA University in official competitions.
5. **5% Early Full Tuition Payment Scholarship:** Granted when paying the full academic year tuition fees by **Monday, August 31, 2026**.
